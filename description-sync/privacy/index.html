Description Sync – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Description Sync plays a film's audio description in time with the film, using an iPhone and an Apple TV. It is made by Mohammad Aloufi.

Description Sync does not collect any information about you.

No data collection

The apps do not collect, store, send or share any personal information, usage data or device identifiers.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The apps do not track you across apps or websites.

Your soundtracks and tracks stay with you

A soundtrack you add stays on your iPhone, which makes its description track there and then deletes its copy of the soundtrack. Tracks go only to your own Apple TV, over your home network, encrypted. They are kept only in the apps' own storage, and are included in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup, only if you use them. The developer never receives them. Deleting the apps deletes them.

The microphone

During a film, the microphone listens so that the description stays in time. The sound is never recorded or stored. It never leaves your iPhone: only fingerprints of it go to your own Apple TV, encrypted.

No servers

The iPhone and the Apple TV talk only to each other, on your local network. The apps do not connect to any server. Your iPhone may download Apple's on-device speech recognition model from Apple, and it then runs on the iPhone.

Test versions

Test versions from TestFlight keep a log of what the apps did and when, on your devices, for two weeks. It never includes sound, and it leaves your device only if you choose Send logs and share it yourself. The App Store version keeps no logs.

Apple's services

If you install Description Sync through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. Apple may pass on crash reports and feedback that you choose to send, and app usage statistics if you have agreed to share them with developers.

Children

Because the apps collect nothing, they collect nothing from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
