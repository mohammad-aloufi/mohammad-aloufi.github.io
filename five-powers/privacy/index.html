Five Powers – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Five Powers is a World War II strategy game for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Mohammad Aloufi.

Five Powers does not collect any information about you.

No data collection

The game does not collect, store, send or share any personal information, usage data or device identifiers.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code. The game does not track you across apps or websites.

Your games stay on your device

Saved games and settings are kept only in the game's own storage on your device. They are included in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup, only if you use them, and the developer never receives them. Deleting the game deletes them.

No internet connection

The game works entirely offline and does not connect to any server.

Apple's services

If you install Five Powers through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. Apple may pass on crash reports and feedback that you choose to send, and app usage statistics if you have agreed to share them with developers.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
